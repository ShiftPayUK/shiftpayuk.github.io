Shift Pay UK

Your shift pay, worked out properly.

Shift Pay UK works out what you'll actually take home from your shifts, using the UK's real income tax, National Insurance, pension and student loan rules, including Scottish tax.

  • Your rota on a calendar, with repeating patterns, overtime, nights, weekends and bank holidays
  • Take-home pay for every payday, weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly
  • Holiday allowance, sick pay, pension and a budget until payday
  • No account and no sign-up: your pay details stay on your phone

Shift Pay Pro is a one-off purchase that removes the ads and unlocks the payslip check, rota import, more than one job, charts, widgets, calendar sync, automatic Google Drive backup and more.
