Taxpot privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 (version 3)

Taxpot is an app for UK sole traders that keeps receipts, mileage, income, invoices and hours for the tax return. This page explains what happens to your information.

In short

Everything you put into Taxpot stays on your phone. We don't run servers that receive your receipts, photos or figures, we don't have accounts, and we don't sell or share your data. If you choose to connect Taxpot to HMRC, the figures you send go straight from your phone to HMRC.

What the app keeps, and where

  • Your records: receipts, receipt photos, trips, income, invoices, quotes, hours and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Receipt reading: receipt photos are read on your phone using Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device. Photos aren't uploaded to read them. ML Kit sends Google basic diagnostics, such as the phone model, app version, how long reading took and any error codes, with a random ID for the install. It never includes your photos, the text read from them or your figures, and Google doesn't pass it to anyone else.
  • Location: only while you're tracking a business trip, between pressing Start and Stop. Taxpot keeps the miles and the names of the start and end places, looked up by your phone. It doesn't keep the route, and it never uses location in the background otherwise.
  • Camera: only to photograph receipts when you choose to.

Sending to HMRC (Making Tax Digital)

This only happens if you connect Taxpot to HMRC, and only when you tap to send something.

  • Signing in: you sign in on HMRC's own website. Taxpot never sees your Government Gateway user ID or password. HMRC gives Taxpot permission to read and send your Self Assessment information, which lasts 18 months unless you disconnect sooner.
  • What's sent: your National Insurance number, and the totals you choose to send: income, expenses grouped the way HMRC asks, Construction Industry Scheme tax already deducted, and your final declaration. Individual receipts, photos and other records are not sent.
  • What comes back: HMRC's list of the updates it expects, its tax calculations and its references for what you sent. These are shown in the app and kept on your phone.
  • Fraud prevention data: the law requires every Making Tax Digital request to carry information that helps HMRC spot fraud. Taxpot sends a random ID made for this install, your phone's make, model and Android version, its screen size, its time zone, its local network (IP) addresses and the app's version. It goes only to HMRC, with each request.
  • The sign-in helper: HMRC's sign-in needs a secret code that can't safely be kept inside an app, so one step goes through a small Taxpot service hosted by Cloudflare. It passes HMRC's one-time sign-in code to HMRC and hands the resulting access back to your phone. It stores nothing, keeps no logs of its own, and none of your figures go through it.
  • On your phone: the access HMRC gives Taxpot is kept encrypted, and isn't included in backups. Tapping Disconnect deletes it. You can also remove Taxpot's access from your HMRC online account at any time.

Once HMRC has your information, HMRC's own privacy notice covers how it uses it.

Backups

When you choose Back up, Taxpot saves a file with your records and photos to the place you pick, such as Google Drive. That file is yours, and we can't see it. Android may also include your records (not photos) in your phone's own Google backup, which you control in your phone's settings.

Sharing

Taxpot only sends something when you ask it to, such as sharing an invoice PDF by email or WhatsApp, or exporting figures for your accountant. Those go through the apps you choose.

Payments

Taxpot Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we only learn whether you have an active subscription. Google's privacy policy covers the payment.

Reviews

Now and then the app may show Google Play's own review card. Anything you write there goes to Google Play.

Children

Taxpot is meant for adults running a business, and isn't aimed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Taxpot, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it keeps on your phone. Anything you already sent to HMRC stays with HMRC, as part of your tax records. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.
