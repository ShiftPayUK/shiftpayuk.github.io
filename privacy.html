Shift Pay UK privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Shift Pay UK ("the app") is made by Simon Blyth. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. If you have a question, email shiftpayuk@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your shifts, pay, tax details and settings stay on your phone. There is no Shift Pay UK account and no Shift Pay UK server, so we never see them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • If you buy "Shift Pay Pro" (previously called "Remove ads"), Google Play and RevenueCat handle the purchase.
  • Backups only go where you choose to send them.

Information kept on your phone

The app stores the shifts, rotas, pay rates, tax code, pension, student loan, holiday, sick day, budget, savings goal and payslip figures you enter, along with your settings. It keeps this in the app's private storage on your phone. Android may include it in your phone's own Google backup if you have that switched on. The app also keeps its own daily backup copy in the same private storage.

Deleting the app deletes this information, unless you made a backup.

Photos and the camera

When you choose to read a payslip or rota from a photo, the app opens your camera or photo picker. The text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit. The photo is not uploaded and is not kept by the app.

Information that leaves your phone

  • Ads (free version). Google AdMob shows banner ads and may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads. It uses these to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Purchases. Buying "Shift Pay Pro" goes through Google Play. RevenueCat checks your purchase so the app knows to hide the ads and unlock the Pro tools. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase details, not your name or pay information. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Bank holidays. The app downloads the UK bank holiday list from gov.uk. No personal information is sent.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google and the app saves backup files to a folder in your own Drive. It asks only for access to files it creates, and cannot see anything else in your Drive. The backups go straight from your phone to your Drive; we never receive them. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.
  • Sharing and exports. When you share a rota, payslip estimate, PDF, spreadsheet or calendar file, it goes only to the app or person you choose.
  • Calendar sync (optional). If you turn on calendar sync, the app adds your shifts to a calendar on your phone. Your calendar provider may then sync them.
  • Email support. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

We do not sell your information. We do not use analytics or tracking beyond the ads described above.

Notifications, widgets and fingerprint lock

Shift, payday and new tax year reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved. Home screen widgets show figures from the app's own storage on your phone (hidden when privacy mode is on). If you turn on the fingerprint or face lock, Android checks it. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.

Crash reports

The app does not include its own analytics or crash reporting. If the app crashes, Android and Google Play may send Google an anonymous crash report, depending on your phone's settings. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is for working adults and is not aimed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can view, change or delete everything you've entered in the app. Settings › Reset app deletes it all. To stop ads collecting your advertising ID, buy "Shift Pay Pro" or delete the ID in your phone's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
