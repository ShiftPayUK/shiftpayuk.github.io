Taxpot privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Taxpot is an app for UK sole traders that keeps receipts, mileage, income, invoices and hours for the tax return. This page explains what happens to your information.

In short

Everything you put into Taxpot stays on your phone. We don't run servers that receive your receipts, photos or figures, we don't have accounts, and we don't sell or share your data.

What the app keeps, and where

  • Your records: receipts, receipt photos, trips, income, invoices, quotes, hours and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Receipt reading: receipt photos are read on your phone using Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device. Photos aren't uploaded to read them.
  • Location: only while you're tracking a business trip, between pressing Start and Stop. Taxpot keeps the miles and the names of the start and end places, looked up by your phone. It doesn't keep the route, and it never uses location in the background otherwise.
  • Camera: only to photograph receipts when you choose to.

Backups

When you choose Back up, Taxpot saves a file with your records and photos to the place you pick, such as Google Drive. That file is yours, and we can't see it. Android may also include your records (not photos) in your phone's own Google backup, which you control in your phone's settings.

Sharing

Taxpot only sends something when you ask it to, such as sharing an invoice PDF by email or WhatsApp, or exporting figures for your accountant. Those go through the apps you choose.

Payments

Taxpot Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we only learn whether you have an active subscription. Google's privacy policy covers the payment.

Reviews

Now and then the app may show Google Play's own review card. Anything you write there goes to Google Play.

Children

Taxpot is meant for adults running a business, and isn't aimed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Taxpot, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it keeps on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
