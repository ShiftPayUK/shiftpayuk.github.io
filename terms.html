What Taxpot does

Taxpot helps UK sole traders keep records of receipts, mileage, income, invoices and hours, estimates the tax they may owe, and, if they choose, sends quarterly updates and other Making Tax Digital information to HMRC.

It isn't tax advice

Taxpot's estimates, expense categories and guidance are general information to help you keep good records. They aren't financial, tax or legal advice, and they can't take every circumstance into account. If you're unsure about something, ask an accountant or HMRC.

You're responsible for your tax

  • The figures you keep in Taxpot and send to HMRC are your own. Please check them before you send anything.
  • You're responsible for meeting HMRC's deadlines and paying the right amount of tax, even if you use Taxpot to send your updates.
  • HMRC's own calculation is the one that counts. Taxpot's estimates and HMRC's in-year calculations are only based on what's been recorded or sent so far, and may change.

Connecting to HMRC

  • You sign in on HMRC's website, and you can disconnect in the app or remove Taxpot's access in your HMRC online account at any time.
  • Only connect your own HMRC account, or one you're authorised to act for.
  • HMRC's services are run by HMRC, not by us. If they're unavailable, or HMRC rejects something, Taxpot will tell you what HMRC said, but we can't make HMRC accept it.
  • Taxpot doesn't yet send the end-of-year final declaration. Until it does, finish your yearly return with your accountant or other HMRC-recognised software.

Your records

Your records are kept on your phone, as explained in our privacy policy. HMRC expects you to keep business records for at least 5 years after the 31 January filing deadline. Please make backups (Taxpot can save one for you), because if your phone is lost or reset, we can't recover your data.
